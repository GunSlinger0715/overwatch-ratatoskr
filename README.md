# Why Ratatoskr Exists

Operational intelligence is only valuable if it reaches the right destination.

OVERWATCH consists of multiple independent subsystems, each responsible for a specific stage of the operational intelligence pipeline. Those subsystems must exchange information quickly, reliably, and without losing integrity.

Ratatoskr exists to securely transport structured operational intelligence between every component of the OVERWATCH ecosystem.

Inspired by the messenger squirrel of Norse mythology that carried messages throughout Yggdrasil, Ratatoskr serves as the communication backbone of the platform.

It ensures that observations become intelligence, intelligence becomes knowledge, and knowledge reaches the systems that need it.

---

# Mission

Ratatoskr provides secure, reliable, and observable communication throughout the OVERWATCH ecosystem.

Its purpose is simple:

Deliver trusted operational intelligence between every subsystem.

# Reliability Principles

Ratatoskr is engineered around several communication principles.

## At-Least-Once Delivery

Messages should never disappear.

---

## Integrity Preservation

Operational intelligence must never be modified during transport.

---

## Ordered Delivery

When required, message ordering is maintained.

---

## Delivery Confirmation

Every successful transmission can be confirmed.

---

## Observability

Every message can be tracked from sender to receiver.
